# English AI patterns

Same idea as the Russian list: what it looks like and how to rewrite. Examples show direction, not copy to reuse.

## 1. Em dashes and dash-built sentences

Before: Meal prep — finally easy.
After: Cook once on Sunday and lunch is sorted until Thursday.

## 2. Colon-built sentences

Before: The secret: batch your emails.
After: I answer email twice a day now, and the inbox stopped running my morning.

## 3. "No X. No Y." chains

Never, not even as one line.

Before: No queues. No paperwork. No fees.
Before: No cash, no cards.
After: Pay with your phone and the receipt lands in your email.

## 4. "Not just X, it's Y" and relatives

Markers: not just, not only… but also, it's not about X, it's about Y, this isn't X, it's Y, more than just, less X and more Y.

Before: It's not just a to-do app, it's your command center.
After: It's the app I open when I have 40 tasks and ten minutes.

## 5. Rule of three

Before: Fast, secure, and private.
After: Your files stay locked even if someone grabs your laptop.

## 6. AI openers and filler

Cut or rewrite:

here's the thing, here's why, here's the kicker, here's the truth, let's dive in, let's dive into, dive into, delve, let's break it down, let's talk about, let's be honest, in today's world, in today's fast-paced, in a world where, in the ever-evolving, imagine this, picture this, the truth is, spoiler alert, buckle up, without further ado, it's worth noting, it is important to note, needless to say, at the end of the day, moreover, furthermore, additionally, in conclusion, to sum up, whether you're a… or a…, look no further.

## 7. Hype words

game-changer, game changing, revolutionary, cutting-edge, seamless, seamlessly, effortless, effortlessly, unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, supercharge, next-level, take it to the next level, transform the way, robust, leverage, harness, navigate (abstract), landscape (abstract), realm, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, vital, ultimate, unparalleled, world of possibilities, journey (abstract).

## 8. Question-answer gimmick

Before: The result? 3 hours saved a week.
Before: The best part? It's free.
After: It saved me about three hours a week, and it costs nothing.

## 9. Empty endings

the future is here, the choice is yours, try it and see for yourself, stay connected, what are you waiting for?, your move.

End with something concrete: what to tap, where to find it, what happened next.

## 10. Rhythm and absurdity

Mix short and long sentences. If a line sounds clever but you can't explain it to a friend in one sentence, rewrite it.

Before: Banking, but make it you.
After: A bank card that tells you where the money went before you ask.
